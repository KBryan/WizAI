## The Demo (5 minutes)

### 1. Dashboard Overview

**Navigate to:** `http://localhost:3000/dashboard-poc.html`

"This is MarketPulse. Notice how clean and professional it looks - this is what your clients will see."

**Point out:**
- Real-time stats at the top
- Last updated timestamp
- Professional dark theme

### 2. Market Overview

"Right away, you can see:
- Average price: $875,000 (up 3.8%)
- Active listings: 847 (down 12% - seller's market!)
- Days on market: 11.3 days
- Price per sqft: $568

This gives you instant talking points for any conversation."

### 3. Price Trends Chart

"Look at this 6-month trend. You can see prices steadily climbing from $845K to $875K. This visual alone helps you explain market conditions to clients in seconds, not minutes."

**Click on different time periods:**
- 6M view
- 1Y view
- Show how it updates instantly

### 4. Municipality Comparison

"Here's where it gets powerful. You can compare all Durham Region municipalities at a glance:

- Brooklin: $1,125,000 average
- Whitby: $965,000
- Ajax: $945,000
- Oshawa: $785,000 (best value!)

Notice the trends - Brooklin is up 6.2%, showing strong appreciation."

### 5. Recent Sales Table

"All recent sales, searchable and filterable. You can:
- Search by address
- Filter by property type
- Sort by any column
- See days on market

This is your instant comp database."

### 6. The CMA Feature (MOST IMPORTANT)

**Click: "Run CMA" button**

"Watch this - this is where you save 2 hours per listing."

**Show:**
- Property address auto-filled
- Bedrooms/bathrooms dropdowns
- Click "Generate CMA"

"In seconds, you get:
- Estimated market value: $985,000
- Range: $945,000 - $995,000
- 3 comparable sales
- Property details

Compare this to your current process. What takes you 2 hours now takes 10 seconds."

### 7. Export Client Report

**Click: "Export Report"**

"One click generates a professional, branded report you can email to clients immediately. It includes:
- Executive summary
- Market statistics
- Trends analysis
- Municipality breakdown
- Your recommendations

Your clients will think you're a data wizard."
